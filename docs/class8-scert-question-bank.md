# Class 8 question bank: straight-line shapes and cubes/cuboids

Source: Telangana SCERT *Mathematics, Class VIII* (2020-21, English medium).
Only questions about straight-line shapes (triangles, quadrilaterals, polygons) and cubes/cuboids are included.
Circles (Ex 9.2), statistics, algebra and number chapters are left out.

Questions are paraphrased. Every answer below was recomputed (not copied from the book).
"Fits grid" means the figure can be built as-is on the app's 20 cm × 20 cm, 1 cm grid.

Legend: ✅ maps onto an existing topic · 🆕 needs a new topic · 📐 needs a new feature (see the end)

## What it would take

**Fits today (✅):** about 25 questions fit topics that already exist, mainly cube volume, cube surface area, cube word problems, cube roots, Euler/nets, frames and congruence.

**New topics needed (🆕):**
1. **Cuboid** (already in the registry as "coming soon"): LSA, TSA, volume, hollow box, tanks. This is the biggest group, about 20 questions.
2. **Quadrilateral, trapezium and rhombus area:** Ex 9.1 Q3, Q6 and Q7.
3. **Similarity:** Ex 8.1 Q6–Q8.

**New features needed (📐):**
- **Scale:** most book sizes (28 cm, 60 cm, metres) don't fit a 20 cm grid. Option: a scale per question, e.g. "1 grid square = 5 cm" or "= 1 m", with labels showing real units.
- **Decimals and litres:** answers like 112.996 m³ and 13 920 L need the litre unit and decimal tolerance.
- **More polyhedra:** prisms and pyramids for the Euler questions beyond cube and cuboid.
- **Figure-based questions** need the book's diagrams redrawn as data.
